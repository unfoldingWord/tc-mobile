# Privacy Policy — translationCore Mobile

**Last updated:** 2026-10-01

translationCore Mobile ("tC Mobile", "the app") is a free, open-source app from
unfoldingWord for recording, editing and sharing oral Bible translation. This
policy covers the app on Android (Google Play and the APK), on iPhone (the App
Store and TestFlight), and in a web browser.

## In short

- **Your recordings stay on your device.** The app has no accounts and no server
  of its own.
- **The app collects nothing from you.** It has no analytics, no advertising, no
  tracking and no crash-reporting software. The web version's host sees ordinary
  connection details; see "What leaves your device".
- **Files leave your device only when you share them,** using your device's own
  share menu, to the app or person you choose.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything the app keeps is stored in the app's own storage on your device:

- **Your recordings,** and the names you give your books, chapters and
  segments.
- **A small problem log.** If something goes wrong, the app writes technical
  details of the error, such as the error message and the part of the app
  where it happened. It does not contain your recordings. It stays on your
  device unless you choose to send it (see below).
- **Your display settings,** such as light or dark.

The app does not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number,
location, contacts or photos.

## The microphone

The app asks for microphone access so that it can record your voice. It uses
the microphone only while you are recording, after you tap Record. Recordings
are saved on your device and are not sent anywhere by the app.

## What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device unless you send it:

- **Sharing recordings.** When you tap Share, the app prepares an MP3 (one
  chapter) or a zip of MP3s (a book) and hands it to your device's share menu.
  You choose where it goes, for example a messaging app or email. From then on,
  that app's or person's own privacy practices apply.
- **Sending the problem log.** When you choose to send it, it goes the same way,
  through your device's share menu, to the person or app you choose.

The phone apps (Android and iPhone) run entirely from files installed with the
app. They do not contact an unfoldingWord server, and they work without an
internet connection.

**The web version** is different in one way: your browser downloads the app
from a website hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver it, Cloudflare processes the
standard information any website receives, such as your IP address and browser
type, and may keep request logs for a limited time under its own policies.
Loading or updating the web version contacts that host again. Otherwise it
handles your recordings the same way as the phone apps: they stay in your
browser's storage on your device.

## Deleting your data

- You can delete a recording, a segment or a whole book inside the app.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device. On
  Android, the app's data is excluded from the phone's cloud backup. On
  iPhone, your phone's own backup (for example iCloud) may include the app's
  data, as it does for other apps.
- In the web version, clearing the website's data in your browser deletes it.

We never receive your recordings, the names you give them or the problem log
unless you choose to send them to us, and there is no account to delete. If you
do send us something, ask us at the email or mail address below to delete our
copy. Connection details the web host may log are
described under "What leaves your device".

## Children

The app is made for adult translation teams. It is not directed at children
under 13, and it does not ask anyone for personal information.

## Open source

The app's source code is public, so anyone can check what it does:
https://github.com/unfoldingWord/tc-mobile

## Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and its date.
Changes are recorded in the repository's history.
